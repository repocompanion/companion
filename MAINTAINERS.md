# Maintainers

Repo Companion is currently maintained by:

- **Lucas Costa Oliveira** — repository owner and primary maintainer.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers review contributions, triage security reports, cut releases, and
keep the public documentation accurate. Security reports follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); conduct reports follow
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Adding maintainers

A contributor who has made sustained, high-quality contributions and is trusted
with release and security access may be invited as a maintainer. The existing
maintainer must agree, and the new maintainer should have a clear area of
ownership. Maintainer access is reviewed when someone becomes inactive for an
extended period.
